# Strategy Reference

Supplemental strategy data for `resume-positioning`. The core skill reads this when needed for certification or targeting decisions.

> **These are worked examples, not rules for you.** Replace them with conclusions from your own rejection data (see `/search-strategy`) and record your version in `strategy/positioning.md`.

## Certification & Credential Strategy

If rejections repeatedly flag missing certifications, prioritize by how many target roles each one covers:

| Certification | Roles It Covers | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| CSM (Certified ScrumMaster) | CS Manager, Product Ops, Hybrid roles | ~1 week |
| Prosci Change Management | Enablement, Ops transformation roles | ~3 days online |
| PMP (Project Management Professional) | Program Manager, Director-level | ~2-3 months |
| ITIL 4 Foundation | Service Delivery, Support Operations | ~1 week |

**Until certifications are obtained:** Reference practical equivalents:
- "Applied ITIL-inspired problem management processes" (not "ITIL certified")
- "Designed Agile/Kanban framework for service transitions" (not "CSM certified")
- "Led digital change management programs across a 20-person team" (not "Prosci certified")

### Anti-Patterns
- ❌ Claiming a certification you don't have
- ❌ Omitting methodology experience just because there's no cert — practical application still counts
- ❌ Listing irrelevant certifications (e.g., HubSpot Inbound Marketing for a Service Ops role)

## Role Targeting Guidelines

Sort role families by how directly your experience maps to them. Example for a customer operations leader coming from managed IT services:

### Strong Fit (prioritize)
- **CS Operations / CX Operations Manager** — maps directly to the most recent role
- **Service Delivery Manager / Director** — natural extension of a services background
- **Customer Enablement Manager** — onboarding + training experience is strong here
- **Support Operations Manager** — process design + automation + team leadership

### Moderate Fit (apply selectively)
- **Head of Customer Success** — requires stronger "managed CSMs" framing
- **Director, Customer Success** — needs director-level title emphasis
- **Program Manager, CS/CX** — needs certification framing

### Low Fit (deprioritize)
- **Senior Product Manager** — formal PM experience gap is hard to bridge in a resume
- **Roles outside your evidence base** (e.g., hardware/IoT operations) — no transferable evidence
- **Roles requiring 10+ years with "SaaS" in the requirement** — adjacent-industry years don't ATS-match

# $100M Leads — Distilled Frameworks

Source: Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

---

## Core Concepts

### What Is a Lead?
A lead is a person you can contact. An **engaged lead** is one who has shown interest. A **customer** is one who has paid.

### The Advertising Equation
```
LTGP > CAC
```
- **LTGP**: Lifetime Gross Profit per customer
- **CAC**: Cost to Acquire a Customer (money + time + effort)
- If LTGP > CAC, you can advertise profitably. If not, fix your offer or your economics.

---

## The Core Four — Advertising Activities

Every business gets customers through exactly four activities. You either reach out or put out content; you either do it to people who know you (warm) or people who don't (cold).

| | **1-to-1 (Outreach)** | **1-to-Many (Content/Ads)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm (Know You)** | Warm Outreach | Post Free Content |
| **Cold (Don't Know You)** | Cold Outreach | Run Paid Ads |

**Start with warm outreach.** It's free, fast, and builds skills. Add the others as you scale.

---

## 1. Warm Outreach

Reaching out personally to people who already know you.

### The 10-Step Process
1. Get your contact list (phone, email, social)
2. Pick the platform with the most contacts
3. Post content before reaching out (warm them up)
4. Reach out to 100 people per day
5. Use the ACA framework: **Acknowledge → Compliment → Ask**
6. Personalize—never copy-paste
7. Make an offer using the Value Equation elements
8. Make it easy to say yes (start free)
9. After exhausting one platform, move to the next
10. Keep the list warm with ongoing value

### Benchmarks
- 100 warm reach outs → ~20 replies → ~4 accept free offer → ~1 paying customer
- 500 reach outs/week × $400 product = ~$104K/year

### Gradual Price Increases
1. First 5 clients → free (for testimonials + feedback)
2. Next 5 → 80% off
3. Next 5 → 60% off
4. Keep raising 20% every 5 clients until you find your price

### The 9-Word Re-engagement
When re-engaging a cold lead: "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?"
No images, no links, just a question.

## 2. Post Free Content

Creating public content that attracts an audience of future buyers.

### The Content Unit (Hook → Retain → Reward)
1. **Hook**: Get attention (headline, thumbnail, opening line)
2. **Retain**: Keep attention (story, curiosity, structure)
3. **Reward**: Satisfy the reason they consumed it (insight, entertainment, transformation)

### Topic Categories
- **Far Past**: Life lessons and hard-won wisdom
- **Recent Past**: Stories from this week's meetings, calls, client wins
- **Present**: Ideas as they happen (tweet them publicly)
- **Trending**: Apply your expertise to current events
- **Manufactured**: Create an experience, then document it

### Headlines — The 7 Components
Include at least two: Recency, Relevancy, Celebrity, Proximity, Conflict, Unusual, Ongoing.

### Give-Give-Give-Ask Cadence
- 3 pieces of value content for every 1 offer
- The content builds trust; the offer monetizes it
- The audience is the compounding asset, not the content itself

### Content Volume
More content → faster audience growth → more leads. "You just gotta do more." Volume works.

## 3. Cold Outreach

Reaching out to people who don't know you.

### Key Differences from Warm
- Need a **list** (bought, scraped, built)
- Need **personalization at scale** (software + manual touches)
- Lower response rates — offset with higher volume
- Must establish credibility quickly (case studies, proof)

### The Framework
1. Build a targeted list
2. Personalize the first line (reference their business, problem, situation)
3. Lead with value (free audit, resource, insight)
4. Follow up 5–7 times minimum
5. Track response rates and iterate

### Volume Math
- If 1% convert and you need 10 customers/month → need 1,000 cold outreach per month
- If you can improve personalization to 2% → need 500

---

## 4. Run Paid Ads

Paying platforms to show your offer to targeted audiences.

### Key Principles
- **Test cheap, scale what works**: Start with small daily budgets across multiple creatives
- **Creative > targeting**: The ad itself matters more than the audience settings
- **LTV:CAC ratio**: Aim for 3:1 or better before scaling
- **Retarget warm audiences**: People who engaged with your free content convert better
- **Track everything**: Cost per lead, cost per customer, lifetime value

## Lead Getters — Scaling Beyond Yourself

Once the Core Four work for you personally, get **other people** to do them on your behalf:

| You Do... | Others Do It Via... |
|---|---|
| Warm Outreach | **Customer Referrals** — Your best customers bring you more |
| Post Free Content | **Employees** — Hire people to do what you did |
| Cold Outreach | **Agencies** — Pay specialists to run outreach |
| Run Paid Ads | **Affiliates** — Others promote you for a commission |

### Referral System
- Ask at peak satisfaction (right after results)
- Make it easy (give them the words, the link, the incentive)
- Reward both parties
- Systematize it—don't rely on asking randomly

### Employee Leverage
- Document your process (SOPs)
- Hire for the Core Four activity you're best at
- Train them to 80% of your output at 20% of your cost
- Manage by metrics, not by feel

---

## Key Principles

1. **More leads solves most business problems** — Revenue, cash flow, hiring leverage
2. **Do one Core Four activity well before adding another** — Mastery beats diversification early on
3. **The audience is the asset** — Content disappears; your audience compounds
4. **Engaged leads are worth 10x cold leads** — Warm them before selling
5. **Volume is the shortcut** — More reach outs, more content, more ads = more customers, faster
6. **Advertising is a skill, not a gamble** — Get reps, track ratios, improve over time

# $100M Money Model — Distilled Frameworks

Source: Alex Hormozi, *$100M Lost Chapters*

---

## The $100M Money Model

A system for maximizing revenue per customer through a staged offer pipeline. The goal: make enough money from one customer within 30 days to acquire at least two more.

### Three Stages

```
Stage I: GET CASH         → Attraction Offers (turn strangers into customers)
Stage II: GET MORE CASH   → Upsell & Downsell Offers (make more from each customer faster)
Stage III: GET MOST CASH  → Continuity Offers (maximize lifetime value with recurring revenue)
```

**Critical rule**: Perfect one stage at a time. Don't build a full Money Model on day one — evolve it as each stage becomes reliable.

---

## Stage I: Attraction Offers

The goal is to turn strangers into customers while covering acquisition costs.

### Attraction Offer Types

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Win Your Money Back** | Customer pays upfront, gets money back if they hit specific goals | Pay $500 for a 6-week challenge; hit your goal and get $500 back |
| **Giveaways** | Give something valuable away to create engagement | Free consultation, audit, or sample |
| **Decoy Offer** | A free/cheap DIY option next to your real offer | Free course vs. $16K done-with-you licensing |
| **Buy X Get Y Free** | Bulk purchase incentive | Buy 4 months, get 2 free |
| **Pay Less Now / Pay More Later** | Low entry price that increases over time | $297/mo now locks in a lifetime rate vs. $399/mo later |

### Key Insight: The Decoy Effect
Placing a free DIY option next to your premium offer makes the premium look like a no-brainer. People who choose the free option still become leads for future offers.

---

## Stage II: Upsell Offers

The goal is to boost 30-day profits well above customer acquisition costs.

### Upsell Types

| Type | When to Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Classic Upsell** | Right after the first purchase | "You got X — want the advanced version for Y?" |
| **Menu Upsell** | Multiple complementary products | "Do you want ads management? Sales training? Both?" |
| **Anchor Upsell** | Present high-price option first | Show $10K package first, then the $3K "just the essentials" |
| **Rollover Upsell** | Turn one-time into recurring seamlessly | After their 6 months of prepaid food, auto-renew monthly |

### Timing
Upsell at the moment of **greatest enthusiasm** — right after purchase or right after seeing initial results. "The best time to sell someone is right after they've already bought."

---

## Stage II: Downsell Offers

The goal is to convert people who said no to your main or upsell offer.

### Downsell Types

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Payment Plan Downsell** | Same product, different payment structure | $5K → $1K down + $200/mo for 24 months |
| **Trial With Penalty** | Low-risk trial with commitment afterwards | "$1 for 30 days; cancel anytime or it auto-renews at full price" |
| **Feature Downsell** | Remove features to lower price | Remove done-for-you ads → lower from $800/wk to $500/wk |

### The Seesaw Method
Alternate between payment plan downsells and feature downsells in the same conversation. Each time they say no, adjust one dimension. This makes you very hard to refuse.

### Temperature Check
After two nos, ask: "On a scale of 1–10, how bad do you want this?"
- 8+: Keep payment plan downselling — "We're gonna figure out a way."
- 7 or below: Ask "What would a 10 look like?" — then recombine features.

### Bartering
Offer discounts in exchange for advertising: reviews, testimonials, social posts, referrals. The advertising value often exceeds the discount.

## Stage III: Continuity Offers

The goal is to stack recurring revenue and maximize lifetime customer value.

### Continuity Types

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuity Bonus** | Give a valuable bonus for joining membership | "Get all 40 past newsletters ($15K value) free when you join at $399/mo" |
| **Continuity Discount** | Free time in exchange for commitment | "First year free if you sign a 5-year contract" |
| **Waived Fee** | Large setup fee waived if they commit long-term | "$5K setup fee → waived if you commit to 12 months" |

### Continuity Pricing Ratios
To steer customers toward continuity vs. one-time purchase:

| To Get X% Choosing Continuity | Make Standalone Price... |
|---|---|
| 50% | 1.33x monthly rate |
| 60% | 1.66x monthly rate |
| 70% | 2.0x monthly rate |
| 80% | 2.33x monthly rate |
| 90% | 2.66x monthly rate |

### Retention Tactics
- **Bill weekly, not monthly**: 13 four-week cycles vs. 12 months = 8.3% more revenue for no extra work (41% profit increase at 20% margins)
- **Processing fee pass-through**: "Plus a 3% processing fee" — negligible friction, straight to bottom line
- **Two forms of payment**: Reduces failed payments from expired cards
- **Lifetime discount at churn point**: If average customer leaves at month 4, offer a permanent discount starting month 5
- **Cancellation fee = discount received**: If they got $600 in discounts, cancellation costs $600 — simple, equitable
- **Exit interviews**: Waive cancellation fee for honest feedback — saves ~33% of canceling customers

## Customer Financed Acquisition (CFA)

The holy grail: customers pay for their own acquisition.

```
If 30-Day Cash from Customer > Cost to Acquire Customer
→ You can scale infinitely with customer money
```

### How to Achieve CFA
1. **Attraction Offer** gets upfront cash to cover CAC
2. **Upsell/Downsell** within 30 days pushes more cash above the line
3. **Continuity prepayment** upsells (bulk time at a discount) boost 30-day revenue further
4. When 30-day cash > CAC, every dollar spent on advertising comes back with profit attached

### The Growth Flywheel
```
Better Offer → More Customers → More Cash Per Customer → More Ad Spend → Even More Customers
```

---

## Money Model Construction Steps

1. **Start with an Attraction Offer** — get customers reliably, cover costs
2. **Add an Upsell Offer** — boost 30-day profits above CAC
3. **Add a Downsell Offer** — convert nos into yeses, increase close rate
4. **Add a Continuity Offer** — stack recurring revenue, maximize LTV
5. **Raise prices in stages** — cheap first for feedback, then raise every 5 clients
6. **Perfect one offer at a time** — don't build the whole model at once

### Key Principle
"Patience is still the fastest." Make each stage reliable before adding the next. When the Money Model starts working, the business starts breaking — operations must scale with revenue.
